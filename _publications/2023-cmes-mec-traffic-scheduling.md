Nowadays, with the widespread application of the Internet of Things (IoT), mobile devices are renovating our lives and the data generated has reached a massive level. Mobile Edge Computing (MEC) has been proposed to solve the limitations of traditional centralized processing. However, how to schedule tasks among edge servers becomes a challenge. In this paper, we design an efficient intelligent algorithm to jointly optimize energy cost and computing resource allocation in MEC. We propose a Deep Learning-Based Traffic Scheduling Approach (DLTSA) that translates the scheduling problem into a classification problem. Evaluation demonstrates that DLTSA can reduce energy cost and achieve better performance compared to traditional scheduling algorithms.
