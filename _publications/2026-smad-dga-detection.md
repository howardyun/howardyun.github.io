Domain Generation Algorithms (DGAs) are techniques used by malware to algorithmically generate large numbers of domain names for establishing command-and-control (C&amp;C) communication. We propose SMAD, a DGA detection approach designed to improve robustness against adversarially crafted domains and generalization to previously unseen DGA families. SMAD introduces a Shift-Attention mechanism that models character interactions beyond local dependencies, enabling the capture of meaningful structural features within a global context. Experimental results show that SMAD achieves up to 96% detection accuracy in open-world scenarios and significantly outperforms state-of-the-art baselines in adversarial detection with an AUC of 0.72, while sustaining over 500 QPS with millisecond-level inference latency in real-world deployment.
