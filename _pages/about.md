I am a Ph.D. student at [Beijing University of Posts and Telecommunications (BUPT)](https://www.bupt.edu.cn/), Beijing, China.

My research interests lie in **AI for Network Security** and **software/model supply chain security**. I am particularly interested in leveraging artificial intelligence techniques to address security challenges in modern networked systems, as well as understanding and mitigating security risks in software supply chains and machine learning model ecosystems.

## Research Interests

- AI for Network Security
- Software Supply Chain Security
- Model Supply Chain Security
- Machine Learning Security
